How TruthLens Evaluates Information

Unlike simplistic keyword searchers, TruthLens evaluates articles through multi-layered natural language signals to detect syntactic anomalies, deceptive framing, and scientific hedging.

Stylistic Patterns

Extracts handcrafted signals: uppercase shouting ratios, punctuation excess (exclamation misuse), and sentence structure irregularities common in deceptive reporting.

Our Commitment to Responsible AI Communication

TruthLens outputs statistical confidence and linguistic flags. Model confidence represents the internal probabilistic certainty of learned stylistic patterns—it does not constitute absolute proof of factual truth. Users are encouraged to cross-reference primary sources and established fact-checking outlets (AP News, Reuters, BBC Verify).
